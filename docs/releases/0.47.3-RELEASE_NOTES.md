# CombatSolver 0.47.3

## 简体中文

- 新增「终局战自动无视卡遗物、卡成长」（默认开启，设置 > 常规 > 幕末 Boss）：最终 Boss 战之后没有下一场战斗，卡遗物计数、局外成长和金币收益在那一场都无法兑现。开启后该场自动按「不考虑局外收益」处理——不再拖延回合凑遗物计数、不再刷成长，也不为这些收益多付血。
- 自动无视只命中整局真正的最后一场：A10 双 Boss 模式下第一只 Boss 之后还有第二只，计数与成长照常参与；第二只 Boss 才自动无视。手动填写的额度与规则原样保留，下一场战斗照常生效。
- 终局战时，成长策略与遗物计数策略面板会显示自动无视提示并灰掉对应配置，让「本场为什么没按计数找路线」看得见。

## English

- Added "Auto-ignore relics and growth on the final boss" (on by default, Settings > General > Act bosses): nothing follows the final boss fight, so relic counters, out-of-combat growth and gold gains can never pay off there. While it is on, that fight is treated as if "Ignore out-of-combat gains" were enabled — no more stalling turns to align relic counters or farming growth, and no HP paid for gains that can never be realized.
- The auto-ignore targets exactly the run's true last fight: on Ascension 10+ double-boss finales the first boss still keeps relic counters and growth active because the second boss follows; only the second one is ignored. Your configured budgets and rules are kept as-is and apply again from the next fight.
- During the run-ending fight, the growth and relic counter strategy panels show an auto-ignore note and grey out the affected settings, making it visible why the route no longer aligns counters.
